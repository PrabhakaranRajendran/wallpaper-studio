Wallpaper Studio 1.1.0

- New Aqua look, plus Ocean, Mint, Sunset, Mono and Material You themes (Settings > Theme)
- Folders tab: set each folder for the Home screen, Lock screen or both
- Add photos straight from Gallery / Google Photos; they're copied into your folder for you
- Home shows what's really on your Home and Lock screens (photos, video, 3D)
- Video: sound on/off switch
- Battery: 3D pauses in Battery Saver or below 20%, video can freeze in Battery Saver, 3D motion capped at 30 fps
- New app icon, and you can add a home-screen shortcut with your own picture
